Security and compliance

Security is part of the architecture, not an afterthought.

If your product handles sensitive information, you need to control who can access it and how it is handled. We design for that from the first decision.

  • Data protection

    Encryption in transit and at rest, with careful handling of sensitive information.

  • Access control

    Role-based permissions and row-level policies that define who sees what.

  • Auditability

    Traceable critical actions and monitoring designed into operational workflows.

  • POPIA awareness

    Collection, access and retention considered in the South African regulatory context.

FAQ

Practical questions, answered plainly.

How does a project start?

With a short conversation about the problem, the people involved and the outcome you need. We then propose a scope, plan and estimate for you to review before anything is committed.

Can you work with our existing systems and team?

Yes. We integrate with existing tools, data and teams, and can modernise ageing systems in stages so operations keep running.

How do you handle security and POPIA?

Access control, encryption and auditability are designed in from the start, and data collection and retention are considered in the South African regulatory context. Responsibilities are agreed with you before work begins.

What do you build with?

Technology follows the problem. We commonly use React, ASP.NET Core, PostgreSQL and Supabase with modern CI/CD, and recommend what fits your context.

Do we own the code?

Ownership, documentation and hand-over are agreed in the engagement terms up front, so you are never locked in.

What happens after launch?

We can stay on for support, monitoring and iteration, or hand over cleanly to your own team, with documentation either way.
